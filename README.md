### What is Sonic Tri-Drive?
Sonic Tri-Drive is a Sonic The Hedgehog fan game, developed by myself. The game is a 2D platformer, which takes inspiration from a few places in the franchise of official games - in terms of gameplay, my project will be akin to Sonic Rush (2005), Shadow Generations (2024), and Sonic Colour DS (2011). In terms of features, I will be lifting ideas from games such as SONIC THE HEDGEHOG (2006), the classic Sonic The Hedgehog games, and Sonic Mania (2017).

The game will feature 3 main gameplay characters; Sonic, Shadow and Silver - each will have the same base controls, but have slight stat adjustment, and character-exclusive moves i.e., Sonic having a Spin Dash, Shadow having his Doom Powers, and Silver having his Telekinesis abilities.

The game itself will feature 10 main levels - all with 1 act each. Each character is being written with the assumption that each level takes place at the same time i.e., Sonic's level 2 and Silver's level 2 both take place at the same time, so Sonic and Silver collide in level 2 of the game. Each level will have a 'sandwich' style of level design - 3 distinct paths intertwining with one another. This diagram will explain more visually:

  <img src="Level Diagram.png" alt="Level Design Diagram" width="600"/>

while a crude diagram - the idea is that the level remains the same, but there are 3 paths in said level that intertwine, which will be accessible by all 3 characters, but one path will be distinctly 'for' a character. For example:
- Sonic's path - linear, speed-oriented, fast paced gameplay
- Shadow's path - lots of enemies, more combat, less platforming
- Silver's path - platforming, slower overall speed, less enemies, more collectables and puzzles

While each 'section' is catered toward a specific player character, each character can access and play the other's sections. For example:
- Silver - has a puzzle to build a bridge to clear a gap
	- Sonic - can clear the gap with a well-timed bounce jump or Boost
	- Shadow - can clear the gap using his Chaos Snap ability with a well placed running jump and quick-time reaction

### Story
*NOTE - this story is from Sonic's Level order, and so will follow Sonic, and then explain where Shadow and Silver are at this time.*
#### Prelude
We begin with Silver in the near future - once again ruined. Silver is eager to go back in time to find the person responsible for this devastated future, and does so after figuring out only one person could be responsible for this level of destruction - none other than Dr. Eggman!

In the present, Dr. Eggman is on the run after another one of his schemes has been foiled by his arch nemesis; Sonic the Hedgehog. The chase is on, and Sonic is in hot pursuit.

Shadow has been tasked with collecting data on Dr. Eggman to build a case on him and his latest scheme. He is actively behind enemy lines collecting his information to bring back to G.U.N.

### Phase 1: The Chase
Pick up with Sonic chasing down Dr. Eggman in the Fable Forest. Eggman has a secret facility deep in the jungle, and is actively fleeing. Sonic, hot on his tail catches up in no time, only to be met with Silver the Hedgehog already there before him. Confused and disoriented by his newly ruined future - Silver attacks Sonic, allowing Eggman to sneak away.

After the fight ends, the ground beneath them begins to shake - out of nowhere, the Flying Battery II raises them into the air - Dr. Eggman has began his escape! With no time to spare, Sonic runs off, leaving Silver to himself while Sonic goes after Eggman. However, Eggman was prepared for such a thing, and after defeating Sonic, he plummets to to Earth, and he gets away into space to his space station - the E.G.G. S.T.A.R.

### Phase 2: The Race to Space
After his not-so gracious re-entry to Earth, Sonic finds himself in the Station Square district - specifically up in the ski resort known as Emerald Resort. He makes his way back to civilisation, passing through the entire resort and back to Station Square, all in an effort to make it to the harbour where he can hitch a ride on a G.U.N. rocket up to space.

Once he makes it to the harbour, it's not long before he finds trouble. None other than Shadow The Hedgehog is set to catch that same shuttle up to space, and so Sonic and Shadow have a little race - neither of them wanting to be stopped from stopping Dr. Eggman's plan. Ultimately, after the shuttle has taken off, Sonic comes out on top, sending Shadow back down to earth.

### Phase 3: Space!
Sonic reaches the E..G.G. S.T.A.R., and confronts Dr. Eggman. With seamingly nothing left, Eggman turns himself in - only to press a button that begins blaring red lights and sirens. Sonic's feet give out beneath him and he begins soaring to the ceiling. Eggman quickly gets away - the E.G.G. S.T.A.R. is coming down!

Sonic gets out of the behemoth of a space station as it falls into orbit, and must find Eggman before it crashes down to Earth, wiping out Station Square. He tackles through debris, saving anyone he can on his way to find Eggman. Eventually, Dr. Eggman is cornered - Sonic, Shadow and Silver all converge. Without another option, Dr. Eggman attempts to flee to space once more - however, it doesn't work this time. Sonic - with all 7 Chaos Emeralds - transforms into Super Sonic and chases him into the stratosphere, closely followed by Shadow with his Doom Wings and Silver with his ESP form. All 3 take down Dr. Eggman in a Final Escape. 

Once Dr. Eggman is defeated, we return to Earth, now rid of Eggman's scheming, and Silver's world is back to being a good future once more!

### Levels and Story Collisions
There will be 10 levels that each character will be able to go through - with 1 act only. There will also be 2 boss fights for each character - themselves. The levels consist of (not in order):
- Fable Forest - Overgrown, dense forest/jungle with castle elements (towers, structures).
- E.G.G.L.A.B - Eggman's remote base within the Fable Forest
- Flying Battery II - Eggman's secret Flying Battery-style blimp that takes off from Fable Forest
- Emerald Resort - Ski Mountain resort in the high mountains of Station Square, with a system of ice caves below
- Station Square - the home city of Sonic Adventure and Sonic Adventure 2
- Rocket-fuel Harbour - Station Square's very own space travel bay along the Emerald Coast
- E.G.G S.T.A.R - The Death Egg sounded too cliche... so Eggman switched it up
- E.G.G-Maggedon - ever wonder what happens when an E.G.G S.T.A.R has to make an emergency landing into a populated city?
- Final Escape - Eggman's on the run once again!
- Final Boss - TBD

# Campaign Narrative Outline
## Core Plot Synopsis
Silver travels back from his ruined future (E.G.G.-Maggedon) to stop Eggman. Meanwhile, Sonic tracks Eggman through Fable Forest, and Shadow conducts a covert investigation. Eggman manipulates the trio, sending space station E.G.G. S.T.A.R. on a collision course with Earth, forcing all three heroes to unite for the final confrontation.

---

## 🔵 Sonic's Campaign (The Pursuit)
- **Level 1: Fable Forest** – Chasing down Eggman after his latest defeat.
- **Level 2: E.G.G.L.A.B.** – Infiltrates Eggman's base and clashes with Silver (**Boss 1: Silver**).
- **Level 3: Flying Battery II** – Boards the ship with Silver; gets knocked back to Earth.
- **Level 4: Emerald Resort** – Survives the drop, lands in the snowy mountains, and treks back toward civilisation.
- **Level 5: Station Square** – Sprinting through the city to reach the coastal space launchpad.
- **Level 6: Rocket-fuel Harbour** – Races Shadow for the last shuttle seat (**Boss 2: Shadow**).
- **Level 7: E.G.G. S.T.A.R.** – Reaches space; Eggman triggers atmospheric self-destruct.
- **Level 8: E.G.G.-Maggedon** – Rescues civilians while racing through the falling space station debris.
- **Level 9: Final Escape** – The final chase into space!
- **Level 10: Final Boss** – Super Sonic, Doom Shadow & ESP Silver vs. Eggman.

---

## 🖤 Shadow's Campaign (The Covert Mission)
- **Level 1: E.G.G.L.A.B.** – Secretly investigating Eggman's master plans.
- **Level 2: Fable Forest** – Escaping back to civilisation with stolen intel.
- **Level 3: Station Square** – Caught off-guard by the Flying Battery II launch; boards the ship.
- **Level 4: Flying Battery II** – Destroys ship defences and hitches a ride to orbit.
- **Level 5: E.G.G. S.T.A.R.** – Finds Eggman, gets trapped, and is banished back to Earth.
- **Level 6: Rocket-fuel Harbour** – Tries to board the second shuttle, but gets outpaced by Sonic (**Boss 1: Sonic**).
- **Level 7: Emerald Resort** – Crash-lands in the ice caves and encounters Silver (**Boss 2: Silver**).
- **Level 8: E.G.G.-Maggedon** – Escapes the Station Square crash zone while keeping tabs on Eggman.
- **Level 9: Final Escape** – Joins the pursuit through the wreckage.
- **Level 10: Final Boss** – Super Sonic, Doom Shadow & ESP Silver vs. Eggman.

---

## ⚪ Silver's Campaign (The Causal Loop)
- **Level 1: E.G.G.-Maggedon** – Ruined future opening; enters time rift to the past.
- **Level 2: E.G.G.L.A.B.** – Meets Sonic in the past and fights over Eggman's whereabouts (**Boss 1: Sonic**).
- **Level 3: Flying Battery II** – Boards the airship and heads straight for space.
- **Level 4: E.G.G. S.T.A.R.** – Finds Eggman, but gets tricked into framing Shadow for the future apocalypse.
- **Level 5: Rocket-fuel Harbour** – Returns to Earth to hunt down Shadow.
- **Level 6: Station Square** – Gathers local intel, placing Shadow in the mountain range.
- **Level 7: Emerald Resort** – Confronts Shadow in the ice caves (**Boss 2: Shadow**).
- **Level 8: Fable Forest** – Realises Eggman's trickery and returns to the forest lab to find him.
- **Level 9: Final Escape** – Catches up with Sonic and Shadow at the crash site for the final pursuit.
- **Level 10: Final Boss** – Super Sonic, Doom Shadow & ESP Silver vs. Eggman.
